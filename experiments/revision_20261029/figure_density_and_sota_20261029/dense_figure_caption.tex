\caption{Per-workflow completion latency under random arrivals in two fresh paired runs of an eight-contract stateful dependency chain. The nominal rate is one workflow per second, with a 30-second continuous preheat and a 360-second observation window at one-second block intervals. Each point is an actual observation-window offer, followed through drain: 363 offers per runtime in R33 and 325 in R34. The horizontal position is its archived scheduled time relative to the observation-window start. Panel (a) retains the full latency scale; panels (b) and (c) repeat the same observations on explicitly enlarged vertical scales. No jitter, synthetic noise, interpolation or additional samples are introduced. EVM latency ends at the sink and NEAR latency at a queryable sink. These are 688 nested workflow observations per runtime from two seed pairs, not 688 independent runtime replications. Migration is excluded.}
